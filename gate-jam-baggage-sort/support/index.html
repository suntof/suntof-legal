Support

Last updated: July 7, 2026

For support, closed-test feedback, privacy requests, or deletion requests related to Gate Jam: Baggage Sort, contact us by email.

Email suntof.apps@gmail.com
Privacy requests Use the same email for access, correction, deletion, and report-removal requests.
Closed test If you are a tester, include your device model, level number, and what happened.

How to contact us

Email suntof.apps@gmail.com. Please include the app name, your device model, Android version if known, and a short description of the issue.

Playtest report files

The app can create an optional JSON playtest report and open the Android share sheet. Send this file only if you are comfortable sharing gameplay diagnostics such as level attempts, moves, blocked taps, hints, restarts, and timing data.

Privacy and deletion requests

If you sent us a playtest report and want it deleted, email us from the same address you used to send the report when possible. We may ask for enough detail to locate the report.

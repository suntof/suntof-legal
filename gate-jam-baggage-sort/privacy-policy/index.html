Gate Jam: Baggage Sort - Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Suntof Solutions handles information for Gate Jam: Baggage Sort, including the current Android internal and closed-test build.

No network permission The current Android build loads local WebView assets and does not declare internet permission.
Local gameplay data Progress, settings, and play history used by the game are stored locally on your device.
Optional test reports Playtest JSON reports are shared only when you choose to export and send them.

1) Who we are

Gate Jam: Baggage Sort is operated by Suntof Solutions. For privacy, support, or deletion requests, contact us at suntof.apps@gmail.com.

2) Scope of this policy

This policy covers the current mobile game build and related testing workflows. It should be reviewed again before adding ads, in-app purchases, remote analytics, crash reporting, cloud save, login, subscriptions, push notifications, or any third-party SDK that changes data collection.

3) Information processed by the current build

  • Local game progress: completed levels, best moves, settings such as sound/music preferences, and similar local game state.
  • Local playtest analytics: gameplay events such as level starts, completions, moves, blocked taps, hints, restarts, durations, and route/test metadata.
  • Optional exported reports: if you tap the export/send test report option, the app creates a JSON playtest report and opens the Android share sheet so you can choose where to send it.
  • Support communications: if you email us, we receive the email address and information you include in your message.

4) Information we do not collect in the current build

  • No account registration or login.
  • No advertising SDK and no advertising identifier permission.
  • No billing or in-app purchase permission.
  • No remote analytics SDK or crash reporting SDK.
  • No precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files access for core gameplay.

5) How information is used

  • To run the game, save progress, and remember settings on your device.
  • To review optional playtest reports that you choose to send.
  • To identify confusing levels, blocked interactions, crashes reported by testers, and polish issues.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

6) Sharing and third parties

The current game build does not send gameplay data to our servers. If you choose to export a playtest report, Android opens the system share sheet and you choose the destination, such as email, Drive, or a messaging app. That chosen service may process the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical web logs when this page is visited.

7) Retention and deletion

  • Local game data remains on your device until you reset progress, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Optional reports you send to us are kept only as long as needed for testing, debugging, and closed-test review.
  • You can request deletion of a report you shared by contacting suntof.apps@gmail.com.

8) Children

Gate Jam: Baggage Sort is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9) Security

We use reasonable safeguards for information we receive through support emails or optional playtest reports. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending sensitive personal information in test reports or support messages.

10) Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant data-flow changes, such as ads, purchases, remote analytics, crash reporting, or cloud features, will require this policy and store disclosures to be updated before release.

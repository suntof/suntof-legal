Help with Prism Mosaic: Gem Sorting.

Contact support

suntof.apps@gmail.com

Email us with your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, level name and a short description. A screenshot or short recording can help. Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Common questions

There is no sound

Open the game's Settings and check Sound and Music separately. Turn up media volume and check whether audio is going to Bluetooth headphones. Return to the game and tap a stone. If sound does not return after an ad or a phone call, close and reopen the game, then report your device and app version.

An ad or the +12 reward is unavailable

Ads need an internet connection and an available ad from the provider. Wait briefly, then try again. The +12 shelf reward requires completing the rewarded ad; opening or closing an ad alone does not grant it. If you complete the ad but do not receive the reward, send us the approximate time and level name. Do not reset your progress.

Protecting my progress

Progress is stored on this device. There is no account-based cloud save or cross-device sync. Do not uninstall the app or clear its data when troubleshooting: this can remove your progress. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for game data. Contact us before trying a reinstall; we cannot promise to recover a deleted local save.

Stones disappear or stop moving

Wait for the current transfer to finish. If the picture still looks wrong, return to the home screen and reopen the level. Report the level name, device model and whether it happened after an ad or returning from another app. A screen recording is useful; do not clear app data.

Where is my completed picture?

Completed pictures are available in your studio collection. Open My Studio, select a wall slot and choose a completed picture. Selecting decor previews it; applying an owned item or buying a new one is a separate action. Coins are used for cosmetic studio decor, not additional power.

The daily picture changed

The daily picture follows the device's local date. Keep date, time and time zone set automatically. Daily and campaign progress are separate. When reporting a daily challenge issue, include its title and the date shown in the game.

Privacy or deletion requests

Use the support email for privacy questions or to request deletion of information you sent us. If possible, write from the same email address. Where available, Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings opens the provider's privacy controls. On iOS, tracking permission can also be managed in the device's Privacy & Security settings.

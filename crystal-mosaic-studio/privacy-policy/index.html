Prism Mosaic - Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Suntof Solutions handles information for Prism Mosaic: Gem Sorting on Android and the web.

Local-first gameplay Progress, settings, coins, boosters, gallery unlocks, and event claims stay on your device. Android backup and device transfer are disabled.
No account required The game does not require registration, login, contacts, location, camera, or microphone access.
Optional rewarded ads When ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent platform process ad-related device and interaction data.

1) Who we are

Prism Mosaic: Gem Sorting is operated by Suntof Solutions. For privacy, support, or deletion requests, contact suntof.apps@gmail.com.

2) Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Android and web builds of Prism Mosaic. The exact SDK and ad configuration can vary by release; material changes to collection or sharing will be reflected here before distribution.

3) Information processed by the current build

  • Local game progress: completed levels, active puzzle state, moves, coins, boosters, gallery pieces, pack rewards, achievements, daily quest state, and weekly event claims.
  • Local settings: sound, motion, dark mode, haptics, symbols, reminders, privacy review state, and local event-log preference.
  • Android backup: cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for local game data in the Android release.
  • Local diagnostics: limited gameplay diagnostics used by test builds remain on the device unless you explicitly send an exported report to support.
  • Support communications: if you email us, we receive the email address and information you include in your message.

4) SDK and network status

The Android app uses Capacitor and may provide optional rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads. Before an ad request, Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether consent or a privacy-options entry point is required. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, diagnostics, and ad interactions to deliver, secure, limit, and measure ads according to the user's choices and Google's policies.

  • No account registration or login in the current release.
  • Rewarded ads are optional and are requested only after the player chooses an ad-labelled reward.
  • No in-app purchase products are active in the current release.
  • No first-party remote gameplay analytics or cloud save is active.
  • No precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files access is required for core gameplay.
  • The Android release denies cleartext HTTP traffic.

5) How information is used

  • To run the game, save progress, and remember settings on your device.
  • To let you export/import local save data.
  • To support local QA and debugging when the optional event log is enabled.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

6) Sharing and third parties

The game does not send gameplay progress to our servers. If you export save or event-log JSON and share it manually, the destination you choose may process that file under its own terms and privacy policy.

When ads are enabled, Google acts as an independent service provider for ad delivery and consent. Review Google's Privacy Policy and use the in-app Ad privacy choices control when it is available. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical web logs when this page is visited.

7) Retention and deletion

  • Local game data remains on your device until you reset progress, clear browser/app data, or uninstall the app. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled.
  • The optional local QA event log can be disabled or cleared from Settings.
  • If you send information to us by email, you can request deletion by contacting suntof.apps@gmail.com.

8) Children

Prism Mosaic is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9) Security

We use reasonable safeguards for information received through support emails. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending sensitive personal information in support messages or exported local JSON files.

10) Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. Significant data-flow changes, such as purchases, remote analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or cloud features, will be reflected in this policy and the applicable store disclosures.
